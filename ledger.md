## Starting state and permission boundaries

At the end of Chapter 9, Cade owes the public a correction. The group has the older testimony, competing witnesses, dated reproduction evidence, and an unresolved copying trail. They have authorized an **inspection**, not the proposed test. No source identity has been established.

Keep three proceedings distinct throughout:

1. **Chapter 6 audio demonstration:** suspended; missing direct recording, safety inquiry, and disputed transcript remain open. Permission to read manuscripts or run a text comparison does not clear it.
2. **Chapter 9–13 text trial:** inspection, supplied commentary, and the agreed source/recording conditions precede Cade's limited authorization. It has no human interface component. Its exact-recovery claim fails in Chapter 13; useful ordinary results remain useful.
3. **Embodiment pilot:** an existing separate program whose history and Jonah's enrollment emerge in Chapters 12–13. Cade's later observation does not authorize the program, certify its doctrine, or erase his objection. The pause in Chapter 15 is a real operational stop, with a defined scope and equipment retained as evidence.

**New logistical choice:** The seized pilot apparatus stays out of use. The proposed public launch uses the partnership's separate presentation/deployment equipment and edited footage; its operators have incomplete accounts of the paused session. Show that distinction when the launch is announced. Kestrel cannot quietly restart an impounded rig, and a local pause does not automatically disable every partner service.

### The Chapter 18 → Chapter 31 chain to show on the page

This is a new concrete implementation of the outline's existing agreement/shutdown mechanism:

1. **Chapter 18:** The agreement delegates specified decisions only after responsible independent operators confirm live validation and applicable participant authorization. Identify their function and the pending activation state here; do not invent a universal legal rule or a shutdown clause at the climax.
2. **Chapters 20–21:** Preserved withdrawal and manipulated validation records undermine those prerequisites. Losing review access complicates delivery, not the existence of the evidence.
3. **Chapters 23–26:** Originals, separate copies, and incident evidence make the case transportable. Public disclosure challenges legitimacy but cannot itself operate equipment.
4. **Chapter 27:** The clinical and technical staff receive the discrepancies and relevant individual decisions. Their knowledge changes before their actions change; their responsibility does not originate in Cade's charisma.
5. **Chapters 28–30:** Release and isolation proceed with assistance for those who need it. A person who stays does not authorize use of everyone else, and protecting that person does not require accepting the doctrine.
6. **Chapter 31:** Operators withhold the previously specified activation, stop this pilot, and interrupt this deployment. The company loses the human participation, infrastructure cooperation, and credible validation required for the scaled operation. SINTAX's continued existence cannot supply those missing conditions by itself.

## Travel, rest, and scene rhythm

- Keep the archive, former repository, and partner facility within the established Rome region. Allow **60–90 minutes per inter-site journey** until specific geography is selected; use on-site work or retained copies instead of rapid unexplained crossings.
- Day 1 includes only the morning facility visit, afternoon repository investigation, and return/safe lodging after the search. Leave the confrontation with Torretti until Day 2. Day 2 allows the return to the installation and an afternoon trial; the bodily pilot waits until Day 3.
- Reserve approximately **seven hours of sleep** on the nights ending Days 1–6, with the shorter final night still allowing roughly six hours before Chapter 26. Off-page intervals contain ordinary care, meals, charging equipment, record preparation, and staff work. Urgency need not imply a week without sleep.
- Day 5's noon session precedes the afternoon repository visit with its own travel allowance. Chapter 24 uses the records already obtained. Day 6 is a full preparation day, not an empty jump to the climax.
- Ruth already appears meaningfully in Chapter 4. Do not add a new appearance merely to fill a ledger cell or compel another lesson about grace.
- Vary the activity: commentary examination → flight with evidence → confrontation → bounded failure → bodily experience → costly pause → recovery → institutional bargaining → humiliation → refusal → exposed complicity → practical help → disclosure → preparation → rescue. Each evidentiary turn must change somebody's next available choice.
