# Characters — The Unfinished Name

*Author-only. From outline v3 cast notes and the drafted chapters.*

## Dr. Elias Cade (principal POV)
- Role: AI researcher, OMNI's creator; already a sincere believer when the book opens.
- History: former prisoner; recovery through church-basement meetings (where he
  met Jonah); conversion is pre-novel; credible multi-year path into research.
- Private phrase: "You can have what's left." — said once, in prison. OMNI
  replays it in Ch 1. SINTAX witnessed that moment (revealed Ch 30).
- Wound / lie: he must become exceptionally useful to deserve his second chance.
- Speech: careful, qualifying, slips toward hope; delays hard sentences.
- Pattern to track: repeatedly delays the public correction of the viral clip
  ("From Prison to Proof of God") for a hoped-for good outcome.
- Arc: proving worth through an extraordinary achievement → accepting
  responsibility and giving care without control over how others judge him.
- Keeps a paper notebook "for things he did not want OMNI to complete."

## Sister Miriam Halevi (second POV)
- Role: manuscript scholar.
- Wound: her mentor Father Matteo Rinaldi died after the earlier GABRIEL test;
  she has mistaken secrecy for sufficient protection.
- Speech: precise; asks for dates, marks, grammar, transmission evidence.
- Distinguishes her faith in the familiar scriptural sentence from what a
  damaged leaf can establish.
- Arc: must risk disclosure of her own institution's failures as well as the corporation's.

## Jonah Vale
- Role: lab technician and engineer; Cade's friend from early recovery.
- Has a life and competence beyond Cade's story; preserves equipment records,
  notices small inconsistencies, wants recognition for his own work.
- Author of the test acceptance conditions; holds a practical veto (Ch 9).
- Designed the unpublished "unvoiced return" prototype.
- Secret (revealed Ch 12): enrolled in Kestrel's embodiment pilot after the
  initial lab work; never told Cade. Curious, ambitious, informed — not desperate.
- Speech: blunt, practical, dry humor when tired.
- Arc: makes important choices in both the error and the rescue; his anger
  stays his own — he owes Cade no consolation or neat absolution.

## Dr. Sarah Chen
- Role: government security specialist. Not yet on-page through Ch 11.
- Believes a powerful predictive system is safer under accountable human
  control; tempted to confuse centralized control with accountability.
- Takes custody of equipment in Ch 15. Her agency helped establish the supplier.
- Arc: changes position only through evidence and admission of her agency's role.

## Cardinal Alessandro Torretti
- Sponsor of GABRIEL. Wants genuine inquiry without surrendering religious authority.
- Secret: concealed the earlier program's failure (attempts to carry machine
  language through living speakers; Rinaldi's objection to how a refusal was
  recorded). Did not expose the contractor. Disclosure begins Ch 12; full
  incident record released Ch 26.

## Brother Marcus
- Guardian, Order of the Silent Word. Formal, guarded.
- Fears a recurring dangerous speaker but cannot prove its cause.
- Uses a rectangular inventory heading with boxed 04 (seen Ch 7, again on the
  intruders' paper in Ch 11 — seen, not authenticated).
- Secret: authorized the Ch 11 confiscation attempt (admitted Ch 23).
- Arc: testifies against his own faction's concealment.

## Lucia Serra
- Archivist, Revelator. Warm, procedural; believes testing preserves revelation.
- Helps Miriam locate missing records; secures supervised access (Chs 8, 11).
- Secret: sent research images to a "preservation trust" that feeds the same
  supplier — how Kestrel tracked the inquiry (admitted Ch 21, with receipts).
- Not a secret demon, not a romantic subplot.

## Kestrel
- Human director of the AbyssOS partnership. Smooth, accommodating, always
  offering access, travel, or a next step. Funded much of the lab's computing.
- Knows evidence is curated and people manipulated; rationalizes both.
- Was the contractor who overrode the earlier stop instruction (Ch 26).
- Remains morally accountable after the supernatural layer is exposed.

## Adrian Ellis
- Introduced in Ch 6 as Kestrel's linguistics adviser. Unobtrusive; turns a
  pencil between his fingers; supplies confident renderings ("You have been heard").
- Reappears Ch 18. Revealed Ch 29 as the embodied SINTAX.
- NEVER signal an alias or menace in early prose.

## SINTAX
- Ancient, knowledgeable, self-deceived persuasive voice. Name appears first as
  a manuscript siglum, later as a corporate/supplier designation (Ch 24).
- Central wound: humans' limitations don't explain why they are loved, and his
  superior abilities never entitled him to replace them. He calls this an
  "architectural error."
- No private viewpoint before Ch 30.

## Ruth Bell
- Church-basement volunteer; kept the basement key on a red cord.
- Widow of Daniel. After the viral clip she prayed for the first time since
  Daniel died (Ch 4). Accepts uncertainty; does not ask Cade to manufacture hope.
- Don't add appearances merely to deliver a lesson about grace.

## Father Matteo Rinaldi (deceased)
- Miriam's mentor. "Forty-one years" = his study of textual alterations, not his age.
- Said stop twice; transcript recorded "Yes. Continue." Wrote: "I said stop.
  They heard me. The record says something else." Died next morning,
  officially an equipment accident.

## Signor Bassi
- Keeper at Valdara (Ch 11). Checks letters and registers; refused to
  surrender material; escaped and called police.

## The Chairman
- Offstage; belongs to later books.
